Pashto Pair Sessions · demo

Try the whole flow before deploying. As admin you create 1:1 sessions; each person opens their own link, types a name, and the two take turns. Screens are in Roman Urdu; speakers speak Pashto. Nothing leaves this page.

This preview is not allowed to use your microphone, so each recording plays soft beeps instead of a voice. On the real platform, and when you open web/demo.html from the package in Chrome, it records your actual voice.
